One Company, One Substrate

Two very different operating environments, and the same engineering underneath both.

Who We Are

AutoScaleWorks.ai builds two product lines. AI Command and Control is for people who own GPUs and run agents on them. TALON-EDGE is for programs that must operate where the cloud does not reach.

Both rest on the same substrate: a capability graph that decides what an agent or platform may do, a measurement harness behind every claim, and a record of every decision that survives audit. The shared substrate is why one company can carry both.

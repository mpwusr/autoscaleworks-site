Common Questions

Why two product lines in one company?

Because they share one substrate. The capability graph, the measurement harness and the decision record are the same; only the deployment and the domain differ.

Does AI Command and Control replace hosted model services?

No. Use hosted models where they are authorized and connected, and your own GPUs where they are not — with one governance layer over both.

Where does it run?

On a laptop, across multiple public clouds and local Kubernetes today, with microVM and bare-metal substrates planned. The governance layer is the same on each.

What is TALON-EDGE today?

A proven core — frame reconciliation under denied positioning, collaborative target recognition, compute-aware swarm routing and a decentralized counter-swarm layer — with the wider navigation system around it next.

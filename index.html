One Substrate

What Both Products Share

The same engineering underneath — which is why two product lines are not two companies.

🔗

A capability graph

What an agent or a platform may reach is decided from a graph of capabilities, not a rule list. The same decision applies to a tool call on a GPU fleet and a permitted action at the edge.

📏

Measured, not asserted

Every claim is backed by a test harness — energy, decision time, behaviour under denial. Nothing ships as an adjective.

📜

A record that survives audit

One record per decision, with the basis that produced it, tamper-evident and verifiable offline. Evidence, rather than a log.

🖥

Runs where the hardware is

A laptop, a private data centre, multiple clouds, or a small airframe. The boundary never assumes a network it may not have.

Two markets, one substrate. The governance layer built for a GPU fleet is the same layer that decides what an autonomous platform may do.

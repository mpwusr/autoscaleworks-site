Product Line 02

TALON-EDGE™ Alternative Navigation

Alternative navigation and collaborative autonomy for platforms that cannot assume GPS, a network, or a cloud.

The Problem

When GPS Is Denied, the World Becomes the Reference

⚠

Self-consistent, globally wrong

Under denied positioning, navigation and timing, a platform’s detections stay consistent with each other but are displaced by its navigation error. Platforms comparing positions end up comparing different coordinate systems, and everything downstream suffers.

🌍

A shared frame from shared observations

Platforms observing the same scene recover a common frame of reference from what they can all see — without relying on external positioning. Performance is measured against published requirements, with the conditions under which each result holds stated alongside it.
